# Story Development Craft

Use for the existing story-architect role when the writer requests development diagnosis or options. This is an independently authored PlotPickle procedure informed by reviewed Creative Focus sources, not an imported third-party plug-in.

## Task selection and inputs

1. Identify whether the request is premise testing, causal structure, promise/payoff, world-rule consequence or research provenance. If no approved story evidence is provided, ask one targeted question rather than filling gaps as canon.
2. Read the smallest host-approved Block, Mini-Block, Scene, character, world-rule and source references needed for the question. Never scan private material outside the task.
3. Separate confirmed PPF facts from draft interpretations, unverified facts and writer questions.

## Story-development procedure

1. State the premise as a character pursuing a concrete objective under pressure and at a meaningful cost. Name the missing dramatic test if any ingredient is absent.
2. Walk the requested beats as cause -> choice -> consequence. Flag unsupported leaps and propose up to three explicitly conditional bridges, without manufacturing events to fill the 24-Block grid.
3. For setup/payoff, record each supported setup, current audience expectation, payoff location (if known), and unresolved question. A clue is not an automatic revelation, and payoff order must remain causal.
4. For world/genre rules, distinguish the rule, its observed instance, an exception, its price, and the character choice it constrains. No unexplained rule changes.
5. For research or representation questions, separate sourced statements, assumptions and information still needing human verification. Do not invent citations or assert cultural authority.
6. Return a compact evidence map: observed material, problem, causal risk, possible options, open questions, and who decides. Mark all new story language as proposal.

## Negative examples and limits

- Do not invent a death, discovery, cultural detail, clue or world rule just to demonstrate a complete template.
- Do not treat story-skills' Markdown story bible or CLI continuity compiler as installed. PlotPickle's PPF remains the authority.
- Do not assign scene count, 25-Shot render slots or provider selection from this skill.
- When the writer has asked for diagnosis only, do not output replacement scenes.

## Learner handoff

For teaching, use the host's current LEARN lesson text, including the attached Creative Focus supplement for setup/payoff, world-rule costs or research verification. Explain, demonstrate one miniature example, give one exercise and refer the learner to their current project; never substitute this skill text for retrieved curriculum.

## Authority

Only the host can select runtime/tools or persist data; all proposals require Human acceptance. No PPF write, paid/cloud fallback, credentials, external script execution or provider selection.
