**Detailed Version**

**Name:** Summer Isobel Ray

**Age:** 41

**Physical Description:** Summer possesses a natural charm that reminds one of Kristin Laura Kreuk. She has dark hair and expressive eyes, and there's an undeniable warmth and energy to her that immediately draws people in.

**Background:** Summer comes from a well-to-do family. Her father was a lawyer, and her mother, a studious bookworm. While they were somewhat controlling, their influence shaped Summer into a determined and independent woman. She has been working two jobs while pursuing her dream of joining a wildlife conservation team.

**Personality:** Summer is outgoing, brave, spontaneous, and light-hearted. She can make friends quickly and is an individual people naturally gravitate towards. Despite her outward jovial demeanor, she is quite reserved and sensitive. She has a guarded heart, carefully concealing her yearning for a genuine connection and love.

**Motivations:** Summer seeks to connect with nature and animals on a deeper level, a desire fueled by the loss of her pet dog. Her dream is to find a balance between city life and nature, with a stronger emphasis on the latter. Her fear is that she might not find what she's looking for - real, authentic love.

**Character Arc:** Summer embarks on a journey, leaving the city behind and heading towards nature. On her journey, she encounters Ren, with whom she forms an unexpected and deep connection. Over time, she lowers her guard, opening up to Ren and realizing her hidden longing for love. By the end of her story, she has transformed from a city-dwelling individual unsure of her need for human connection, to a woman in love and harmony with both nature and another person.

**Relationships:** Apart from her relationship with Ren, Summer shares a significant bond with her robotic pets. These pets represent her past life in the city and her struggle to connect with real animals and nature. Halfway through her journey, she decides to set them free, symbolizing her own liberation.

**Conflicts:** Internally, Summer struggles with opening up her heart due to her controlled upbringing. Externally, she consciously distances herself from the societal norms of the city, desiring to see a loving and empathetic world which often contradicts the reality she faces.

**Strengths & Weaknesses:** Summer is caring, nurturing, and possesses a magnetic personality. Her weakness lies in her distrust of humans, often opting to nurture her robotic pets instead.

**Supporting Characters:** Amy, the AI, serves as the narrator of Summer's story, subtly guiding her relationship with Ren. Summer's occasional friends from the city serve as minor supporting characters.

**Setting Relationships:** The city represents her past, the beach symbolizes transition, and Costa Rica embodies her desire for a life surrounded by nature. These settings play a pivotal role in her journey and personal growth.

Summer Isobel Ray, a 41-year-old city-dweller with an outwardly vibrant personality, is on a journey of self-discovery and connection.
