# PlotPickle trademark and brand policy

The PlotPickle name, PlotPickle Playhouse name, logos, icons, visual marks, and other source-identifying brand assets are used to identify the official PlotPickle project created by Bryan Harris.

Open-source and Creative Commons licences grant copyright permissions. They do not automatically grant permission to use a trademark in a way that creates confusion, implies endorsement, or misrepresents the origin of a modified product.

## Permitted descriptive use

You may truthfully state that software is:

- based on PlotPickle;
- compatible with PlotPickle project files;
- a fork or modified edition of PlotPickle; or
- built using the 24 Blocks method under the applicable licence.

Such use should be factual and should not imply sponsorship or official status.

## Modified editions

A modified public edition should:

- identify itself as modified;
- use a clearly distinguishable product name or modifier;
- state who maintains the modified edition;
- link to its corresponding source when required by AGPLv3; and
- preserve the attribution and licence notices required for PlotPickle software and documentation.

## Restricted use

Without separate written permission, do not:

- present a modified version as the official PlotPickle edition;
- use the PlotPickle logo as the primary identity of an unrelated product or service;
- imply endorsement, certification, partnership, or sponsorship by Bryan Harris;
- register a confusingly similar domain, business name, or product identity; or
- remove notices that distinguish the official project from a modified edition.

## Screenshots and discussion

Screenshots, reviews, tutorials, reporting, commentary, and educational discussion may use the PlotPickle name and interface images in a reasonable nominative manner, subject to applicable law and the licences covering the underlying material.

## Contact and legal note

Requests for broader brand permission should be directed to the official PlotPickle repository owner.

This policy is a practical statement of intended brand use and is not legal advice.
