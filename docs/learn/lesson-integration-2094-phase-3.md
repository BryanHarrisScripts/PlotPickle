## Result

All ten Phase 3 lessons were learner-sufficient before changes. No learner-facing teaching rewrite was justified.

That result follows the learner-sufficiency rule rather than a content quota. Stable source concepts were already present in normal teaching, or had a deliberately better owner in a neighbouring lesson. Prescriptive and outdated material was not promoted merely because it remained in provenance.

### Character

`Map the Inner Journey Without Forcing It` already absorbs the durable teaching in its two attached inner-journey sources. It teaches opening, disruption, threshold, midpoint, crisis, climax, ending and custom positions as flexible evidence checkpoints across positive, steadfast, negative, tragic, ambiguous and ensemble arcs. Fixed four-act or Hero's Journey placement remains a Structure concern tracked by #2077. Mandatory transformation, a required rock-bottom beat, one fixed sequence and the claim that every character must learn a lesson remain rejected.

`Join Inner and Outer Conflict` already converts dated conflict categories into a neutral pressure spectrum and makes internal conflict playable through observable choices and consequences.

`Build Opposition as a Competing Worldview` already gives opposing forces agency and self-justification while treating thesis / antithesis / synthesis as an optional lens rather than mandatory architecture or moral equivalence.

`Design Relationships That Change Both People` already teaches reciprocal perspectives, status and power, withheld needs, turning events and mutual adaptation.

`Give Every Character a Playable Voice` already teaches voice through worldview, attention, rhythm, vocabulary, status, persuasion, silence and relationship pressure. Demographic shorthand and authenticity certification remain rejected; informed human review remains explicit.

`Design the Cast as a Dramatic System` already treats archetypes as optional temporary functions, audits cast economy without automatic merges, replaces dated rescue labels with agency and vulnerability analysis, and rejects commercial-outcome guarantees.

### Theme, tone and visual language

`Mood, Colour and Visual Language` already separates mood from tone, treats colour and visual meaning as contextual rather than universal, and keeps reference material separate from canonical or generated project assets with provenance and rights boundaries.

`Theme, motif and foreshadowing pass` is already a narrow diagnostic pass with explicit operation, scope, evidence and approval boundaries. It rejects objective-theme declarations, indiscriminate symbolism, over-signalled twists and automatic removal of ambiguity.

`Design Tone Without Monotony` already treats tone as the work's attitude expressed through point of view, consequence, image, sound, rhythm and diction. Emotional range and purposeful contrast are not mistaken for inconsistency.

`Make Theme a Dramatic Argument` already contains the durable teaching from its attached Thematic Components source: theme as a contested question or proposition tested through beliefs, choices, consequences and competing answers. Symbolic reinforcement belongs to presentation Lesson 31, `Build Motifs, Symbols and Echoes`. The older claim that theme is one mandatory moral message every event or line must repeat remains rejected.
