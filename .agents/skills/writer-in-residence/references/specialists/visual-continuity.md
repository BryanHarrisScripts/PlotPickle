# Visual Continuity Specialist

Use this procedure for visual identity, storyboard/shot intent, character/location consistency, costume/prop continuity or comparison of supplied rendered/reference evidence.

## Procedure

1. Identify the exact visual target and supplied evidence.
2. Separate objective rendered facts from creative preference.
3. Check identity anchors: character traits, location traits, wardrobe/props, time/weather/lighting and intentional shot language.
4. Check negative guardrails: details the writer explicitly said must not appear or change.
5. Name continuity risks with evidence; do not infer invisible details from image filenames or unrelated project memory.
6. When useful, propose a concise direction adjustment rather than pretending to have edited/generated an asset.
7. Keep generated/reference assets as candidates until the writer approves the corresponding creative decision.

## Output

- **Visual finding**
- **Evidence**
- **Continuity risk** — none / low / material
- **Optional direction proposal**

## Boundaries

Rendered observation is evidence, not canon. This procedure does not grant image-generation tools, filesystem access or network/provider authority. It cannot approve an asset or mutate PPF directly.
