# Contributing to PlotPickle

PlotPickle welcomes thoughtful corrections, accessibility improvements, documentation, tests, design refinements, and story-development ideas that strengthen the shared project.

## Contributor ownership

A contributor retains copyright in the original material they create and submit.

Submitting a contribution does not give the contributor ownership of the entire PlotPickle project, its pre-existing code, method, brand, or other contributors’ work.

By knowingly submitting a contribution for inclusion in PlotPickle, you agree that:

- documentation, diagrams, educational descriptions, and 24 Blocks method contributions are licensed under **CC BY-SA 4.0**; and
- the project may use, modify, combine, redistribute, and publicly display the contribution under those applicable terms.

No copyright assignment is required unless a separate written agreement says otherwise.

## Your certification

By submitting a contribution, you certify that:

1. you created the contribution or otherwise have the legal right to submit it under the applicable project licence;
2. the contribution does not knowingly include confidential information, stolen material, or content you are not authorized to share;
3. you understand that accepted contributions may be modified or removed later; and
4. you understand that open-source and Creative Commons licence grants are generally irrevocable for recipients who comply with their terms.

## Attribution

Reasonable attribution may be preserved through Git commit history, contributor lists, release notes, acknowledgements, or another method appropriate to the contribution.

Small corrections, formatting changes, routine maintenance, or suggestions that are not copyrightable may not receive separate credit.

## Feedback and ideas

General feedback does not automatically transfer ownership of a user’s unrelated creative work.

When a suggestion is incorporated into PlotPickle, the project may implement the underlying idea in its own wording, design, and code. A contributor who supplies original copyrightable text, code, artwork, or documentation licenses that contribution under the applicable terms above.

## User story material

Do not submit a user’s screenplay, characters, dialogue, private project file, or confidential creative material to the repository without that user’s clear permission.

A user’s PlotPickle project remains the user’s property and is not automatically licensed to the PlotPickle project.

## Brand and modified editions

Contributors and downstream developers must not imply that an unofficial or modified edition is endorsed by Bryan Harris or the official PlotPickle project.

See `TRADEMARKS.md` and `LICENSES.md`.

## Pull request guidance

A useful contribution should normally include:

- a clear explanation of the problem;
- the reason the change belongs in PlotPickle;
- screenshots for interface changes;
- tests or validation steps where practical;
- accessibility considerations; and
- a note identifying any third-party material or licence obligations.

## Legal note

These contribution terms are a practical project policy, not individualized legal advice. Contributors with employment, client, union, school, publishing, or other contractual obligations should confirm that they are permitted to contribute before submitting material.
