# Practical outcome measurement protocol

Preparation only. Proposed metric definitions, matched scope and thresholds await Human review in #2943. Actual baseline, candidate results and practical benefit are UNPROVEN. No before-CI baseline is reconstructed from test counts or green runs.

The JSON protocol defines 14 metrics: accepted-truth-to-verified-outcome elapsed time; escaped failures; repeat repairs; unexpected changes; active diagnosis, implementation, contract and verification effort; actual contract/verification cost in CAD; requirement restatements; manual reproductions; active Human time; and unnecessary technical decisions returned to the Human. Counts are per complete episode; time totals include all repairs/attempts, with active effort kept separate from elapsed waiting. Null/missing measurements remain UNKNOWN, never zero. Human judgments distinguish restatements from changed requirements and unnecessary choices from consequential authority/meaning decisions.

The proposed small campaign has four matched episodes per cohort: normal Save/restart, rejected Save with retained work, lost-acknowledgement reconciliation and stale-revision protection. Each episode begins with an equivalent accepted repair/workflow truth and ends with an independently verified outcome plus the next normal restart/Library-reopen follow-up. Register every episode/attempt first; retain failures and incomplete outcomes. Use equivalent disposable workloads and supported same-device/profile conditions; preserve private work. Any unmatched configuration, fault schedule, truth edition or workload invalidates comparison. All outcomes must eventually be verified; blocked/unknown episodes leave the campaign inconclusive rather than disappearing from averages.

Baseline revision is the current reference main after #2939. Candidate revision is currently unknown. Freeze both actual revisions in a reviewed successor protocol before collection. This is a prospective comparison of a bounded subsequent repair campaign, not causal evidence about the entire historical CI transition. Earlier baseline values remain unknown unless a separately reviewed historical protocol and independent records support them.

Proposed thresholds: zero candidate escaped failures/unexpected changes, every candidate episode verified, no increase in any measured metric (including total active engineering/verification effort and each Human burden measure), and a strict decrease in at least one reliability or Human burden measure. Ratios have ceiling 1; when baseline is zero, candidate must also be zero. Timing or cost improvement alone cannot hide unchanged Human frustration. These exploratory criteria are proposed for review, not new approved product promises, population evidence or statistical significance. Amendments require a new approved protocol digest and campaign, never retrospective tuning.

Approval is separate host configuration: PLOTPICKLE_MEASUREMENT_APPROVED_PROTOCOL_DIGEST, PLOTPICKLE_MEASUREMENT_APPROVED_AT and PLOTPICKLE_MEASUREMENT_APPROVAL_REF. Dataset flags cannot approve a protocol. The digest covers all definitions, cases, exact revisions, thresholds and limitations; collection must begin later than approval.
