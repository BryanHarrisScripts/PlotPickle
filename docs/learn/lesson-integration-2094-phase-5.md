### Structure and series craft

`Scenes That Turn and Hand Off` already teaches objective, opposition, action, reversal, turn, consequence and handoff as the scene-level causal chain. The historical source's fixed 120-minute/four-act assumptions, average scene-count claims and routine establishing-shot framing are not promoted as rules. Dialogue tactics remain owned by the Dialogue curriculum.

`Control Pacing Through Change` is source-free and already defines pacing through meaningful change, urgency, information, tactics, reversals, density, silence, transitions and processing time. It explicitly rejects genre-speed stereotypes, constant intensity and length as the only pacing signal.

`Series Craft: Episode and Season Architecture` already separates episode, pilot, season and series scales; teaches A/B/C story weaving, act-outs or sequence endings as pressure points, pilot promise and accumulated season consequence; and explicitly treats current delivery conventions as separately verified production context rather than universal craft law.

`Series Engine and Bible Design` already distinguishes the recurring series engine from the pilot plot, tests episode variety, separates established continuity from future possibilities and keeps the internal creative bible distinct from mutable external presentation requirements.

`Half-Hour Comedy Craft` already teaches comic premise pressure, setup/expectation/turn/payoff, escalation through commitment or consequence, runners/callbacks with variation and form choice after the comic engine. Fixed joke counts and one universal single-camera/multi-camera/distribution formula remain rejected.

### Visual Storytelling

`From Concept Card to Storyboard Frame` already distinguishes legacy visuals, Block covers, storyboard frames and production keyframes; requires side-by-side comparison before approval; and preserves mapping, naming, rights and provenance decisions. The attached Mood Board Collection is primarily reference inventory; its durable visual-language teaching is already owned by Lesson 27, `Mood, Colour and Visual Language`. The attached Video-Retalking setup notes remain historical technical evidence and are not current installation authority.

`Visual action and PageFlow pass` is intentionally a narrow revision pass. It already requires filmable action, readable paragraphing, purposeful visual/sound choices, bounded scope and explicit human approval. The attached `Lost & Found` short-video challenge remains a historical exercise/example; its storyboard, transition and sound-design material is better owned by the broader visual/storyboard lessons rather than duplicated into this diagnostic pass.

`Turn Inner Meaning Into Screen Evidence` is source-free and already teaches conversion of abstract/internal claims into visible, audible or performable evidence, selective detail, intentional narration and the difference between screenplay evidence and production coverage. Routine camera direction, décor inventory and unplayable internal thought remain rejected.

### Drafting

`The Writing Process` already teaches discovery, selection, bounded research, planning, drafting, feedback, layered revision and proofreading as purposeful modes. The historical source's named rough-draft terminology is optional provenance, not a mandatory process.

`Concept to Final Draft` already teaches one causal spine moving from concept and premise through Blocks, mini-blocks, treatment, screenplay and revision, while allowing draft discoveries to update the project source of truth. The source's fixed universal ten-step order is not promoted.
