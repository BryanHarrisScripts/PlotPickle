## 54 — Screenplay Challenges Guide

Original source verified at `24-Blocks/General/Screenplay Challenges Guide.md`.

The current lesson correctly teaches diagnosis from evidence, but the old review over-compressed the source's breadth. The named challenge inventory should remain learner-visible as a reference taxonomy so writers can find a problem even when they do not yet know which deeper lesson owns the solution.

- `RESTORE_REFERENCE` — Story craft: lack of originality; trouble with structure; lack of conflict; pacing; suspense; theme development; clichés; openings/closures; scene transitions; writing action; escalating conflict; incorporating backstory.
- `RESTORE_REFERENCE` — Storytelling form: unreliable narrative; nonlinear storytelling; brevity/efficiency; character arcs; maintaining perspective/big-picture awareness.
- `RESTORE_REFERENCE` — Character: poorly defined characters; empathy; authentic relationships; subtext; conveying emotion; consistent/distinct voice; stereotype avoidance; believable antagonists; character consistency; diverse representation.
- `RESTORE_REFERENCE` — Language/dialogue: compelling dialogue; exposition; show-versus-tell judgment; developing a distinctive style.
- `RESTORE_REFERENCE` — World/genre/production awareness: world-building; visualizing sets/locations; audience/genre expectations; cultural sensitivity; managing scope; genre mixing; changing technology.
- `RESTORE_REFERENCE` — Audience/emotion: conveying emotion without melodrama; sustaining engagement; empathy without requiring likability.
- `RESTORE_REFERENCE` — Writer/process: writer's block; feedback and rejection; revision; finding time; accuracy; avoiding anachronisms; conflicting feedback; solitude; sustaining motivation; writer fatigue; tonal movement between comedy and drama.
- `RESTORE_REFERENCE` — Industry/business questions: genre conventions; changing trends; selling/pitching; art versus commerce; multiple projects; standing out.
- `RESTORE_REFERENCE` — Technical/practical: continuity; research; rights/legal questions; formatting; film-language/technical detail; symbolism; sequencing.
- `MODERNIZED`: rights, registration, contracts, competitions, market trends and legal protection are questions requiring current authoritative information; the old source's specific suggestion that WGA registration or the U.S. Copyright Office is universally the correct protection path is not frozen as global advice.
- `REJECTED`: a hard 120-page ceiling, popularity/commercial success as craft truth, or generic “make it stand out” advice treated as a deterministic repair.
- Formatting target: preserve the taxonomy as grouped bullet sections while keeping PlotPickle's evidence → likely layer → bounded experiment method as the primary teaching.
