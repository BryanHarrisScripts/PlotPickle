# Act I Launch

Act I Launch is PlotPickle's foundation diagnostic for Blocks 1–6. It checks whether the opening act establishes enough story information, opposition, emotional value and irreversible choice to support Blocks 7–24.

The twelve launch signals are Primary Presence, Revealing Contrast, Opposing Pressure, Pressure Multiplier, Disruption Lands, Problem Named, Outside Push, Inner Lock, Counterstrike, Emotional Anchor, Personal Threat and Irreversible Step.

The signals are functions, not fixed page beats. A writer may move, combine or omit a signal when another scene performs the function more effectively. PlotPickle therefore reports the missing function, its downstream consequence and the evidence that could satisfy it rather than demanding conformity.

Opening Move is the first-scene layer inside Act I Launch. Act I setups and unresolved Story Threads become downstream promises that later blocks must develop, transform, pay off or deliberately deny.
