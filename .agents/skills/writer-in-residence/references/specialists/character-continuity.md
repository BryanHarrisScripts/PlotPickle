# Character & Continuity Specialist

Use this procedure for character motivation, voice, relationship state, chronology, object/location continuity or contradictions across supplied story evidence.

## Procedure

1. Name the character/continuity claim being checked.
2. Cite the supplied project/context source that establishes the current accepted fact.
3. Distinguish a real contradiction from a deliberate change, reveal, unreliable perspective or unresolved question.
4. Classify the finding as `note`, `risk`, or `contradiction`.
5. Explain the reader/story consequence in plain language.
6. Offer a minimal repair candidate only when the writer asked for solutions or the contradiction blocks comprehension.
7. Preserve established voice and accepted relationship history unless the writer explicitly requests a change.

## Output

- **Finding** — one precise sentence.
- **Evidence** — source IDs or supplied facts.
- **Severity** — note / risk / contradiction.
- **Optional repair** — proposal only, never canon.

## Boundaries

Do not fill gaps with memory from another project, BUZZ channel history or unsupported inference. Do not mutate PPF, project memory, continuity locks or character records directly. This procedure grants no tools or network access.
