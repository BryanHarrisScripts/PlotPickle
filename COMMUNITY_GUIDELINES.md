# PlotPickle BUZZ Community Guidelines

Last reviewed: 25 August 2026

These guidelines apply to the official PlotPickle Community BBS and its BUZZ rooms. Repository contributions remain subject to `CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md`.

## Write, discuss, and disagree without harming people

PlotPickle is a storytelling environment. Fictional, historical, educational, or analytical work may legitimately discuss drugs, weapons, suicide or self-harm, mass violence, sexuality, profanity, abuse, or other difficult subjects. A sensitive word or difficult fictional event is not misconduct by itself.

Participants may critique ideas, craft, and public work. They must not target people with harassment, bullying, discriminatory abuse, sexualized attention, credible threats, intimidation, doxxing, impersonation, privacy violations, credential sharing, spam, or repeated deliberate disruption.

Real-world sourcing, trafficking, preparation instructions, control-evasion procurement, coercion, operational harm planning, encouragement of current self-harm, or credible threats of mass violence are safety concerns rather than ordinary story discussion.

## Protect creative work and identity

- Share only material you have the authority to share.
- Do not post another person's unpublished story, private room content, identity, contact information, credentials, or private files without permission.
- Do not assume that feedback, room membership, public visibility, or a BUZZ message transfers copyright or creates a licence.
- Treat public-room messages as public to the Community. A private or closed room controls membership but is not automatically a legal confidentiality agreement.
- Never paste API keys, BUZZ private signing keys, recovery secrets, repository tokens, or other credentials into a room.

## Merrin's actual role

Merrin Bellwarden is a visible BUZZ-managed host and contextual guide. Merrin evaluates context rather than applying a banned-word list and should use the least intrusive suitable response: allow, quietly observe, redirect, de-escalate, make a supportive safety intervention, or surface a concern for Human review.

Merrin cannot silently delete messages, ban or block members, alter room permissions, investigate participants, infer or store sensitive personal profiles, read unrestricted PPF state, enter private Story Rooms without explicit membership, change story canon, or make a final enforcement decision.

Human owners, administrators, moderators, and participants may use only the report, block, membership, and room controls that BUZZ and the Community actually provide. PlotPickle does not promise a particular response time, removal result, appeal system, or emergency service.

## Reports and urgent safety

Use the available PlotPickle or BUZZ report/block path and preserve the signed message or event reference when practical. Share only the minimum information needed for review; do not repost harmful or private content into a public room.

For a repository conduct concern, follow `CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md`. For an immediate real-world emergency or current risk of harm, contact the appropriate local emergency or crisis service; Merrin and the PlotPickle project are not emergency responders.

## Human review

Community owners remain responsible for Human judgment and for the controls their BUZZ deployment actually supports. A software agent may assist with context and triage but does not approve legal sufficiency, determine criminality, or replace qualified safety, legal, or mental-health professionals.
