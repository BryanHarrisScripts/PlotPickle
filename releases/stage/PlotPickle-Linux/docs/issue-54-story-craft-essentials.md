## Story Experience Card

The card records anchor experience, audience promise, emotional movement, dominant and secondary genre, tonal promise and range, central question, expected and alternative answers, signature image/sound/movement and ending after-effect. It is a reference rather than a rigid constraint.

## Pacing and Tone Map

The map surfaces duration, pressure evidence, information change, tonal mode, pivots and handoffs across existing scenes. It does not reward constant speed or intensity. A quiet scene may move rapidly through irreversible change; action may feel slow when strategy, cost and information repeat.

## Theme Argument Map

Theme is treated as a contested question tested through competing answers, character strategies, Story Threads, choices, consequences, climax proof and ending reflection. A scene contributes by changing the conditions under which the question will be answered; it does not need to restate the theme.

## Scene Pulse learning overlay

Pressure Lock, Cut Line, Pivot, Value flip and Handoff each include a plain-language definition, reason, current evidence, revision experiment and direct controlling field. The overlay reads existing Scene Pulse diagnostics rather than creating a parallel score.

## Screen Evidence Translator

The translator converts abstract internal claims into selected action/refusal, changed objective/tactic, objects or blocking, sound/silence, dialogue/subtext, repetition with variation and intentional narration. Visual writing selects meaning; it does not inventory every object or dictate routine camera coverage.

## Motif and Echo Ledger

Motifs are tracked through literal introduction, repetitions, meaning shifts, associations, visual/dialogue/sound/music appearances, payoff/reversal/unresolved ending and continuity requirements. Symbols gain meaning through context, culture, repetition and change rather than fixed lookup tables.

## Advanced formatting toolbox

Editable previews cover mini-slugs, montage, series of shots, intercutting, remote conversations, V.O./O.S., dual dialogue, inserts/on-screen text, meaningful transitions, Fountain sections/synopses/notes and boneyards. Every technique documents dramatic purpose, readable form, Fountain/FDX/PDF preservation, common misuse and the appropriate spec, production or internal layer.

## Essential Craft Audit

The fourteen-step audit reviews promise, causality, character agency, pressure, scenes, theme, pacing/tone, evidence, motifs, dialogue, genre, formatting, continuity/production and revision priorities. It records evidence-based questions, a root cause and a bounded next pass. It does not produce a universal screenplay score or an objectively perfect final draft.

## Approval and AI boundaries

Manual and no-AI workflows are complete. Optional AI may help assemble or compare a bounded pass, but output remains non-canonical until explicit writer approval. Cards, maps, ledgers, previews and audits do not rewrite, format or insert story material automatically.
