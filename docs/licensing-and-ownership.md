# Licensing and ownership

PlotPickle separates four different kinds of rights so users, contributors, and server operators can understand exactly what they own and what obligations apply.

## Traditional copyright and open licensing can coexist

Copyright exists automatically in original creative work. An open licence does not erase copyright; it is a standing permission from the copyright holder allowing specified uses under stated conditions.

PlotPickle uses different licences for software and for educational material because they serve different purposes.

- **Software:** GNU Affero General Public License version 3 or later.
- **Written method and documentation:** Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International.
- **User-created stories and project files:** retained by the user or other applicable rights holder.
- **PlotPickle brand identity:** protected against misleading or confusing use.

## Why the software uses AGPL instead of Creative Commons

Creative Commons recommends against using CC licences for computer software because CC licences do not contain software-specific source-code and patent terms.

PlotPickle is designed primarily as a downloadable local-server application, but it can also be adapted for a hosted server environment. AGPLv3 is specifically designed for software that may be used over a network. Its network clause requires operators of modified hosted versions to offer the corresponding source code to the users of that version.

This matches PlotPickle’s intended collaborative model more accurately than applying CC BY-SA directly to the application code.

## Official distribution model

The official PlotPickle edition is distributed as a downloadable package that runs a private local server on the Human's own computer.

The official project does not require an online PlotPickle account or an official cloud-hosted PlotPickle service.

PlotPickle includes an advanced `server-network` mode. A downstream operator may configure it on infrastructure they control, subject to the applicable licences, deployment-security requirements, and accurate operator-supplied privacy and service terms. The official project does not promise compatibility with a particular hosting control panel or CMS.

## Server operators

An operator may run the unmodified software or create a modified edition.

A modified version made available to remote users must:

1. preserve appropriate legal and copyright notices;
2. clearly state that it was modified;
3. provide a practical, no-charge way for remote users to receive the corresponding source code for the version they are using;
4. license the modified software under AGPL-3.0-or-later; and
5. avoid implying that an unofficial edition is endorsed by Bryan Harris or the official PlotPickle project.

Server operators do not gain ownership of user stories merely by providing hosting. Their own privacy policy and terms should clearly explain storage, backups, access controls, retention, and deletion of user data.

## User contributions and ownership

A contributor retains any copyright and other rights they hold in original feedback text, code, artwork, documentation, or other material they create.

General ideas, facts, methods, short phrases, and suggestions may not themselves qualify for copyright protection. When a contribution contains protectable original expression, the contributor retains copyright while licensing that contribution under the applicable PlotPickle licence.

- Code contributions are licensed under AGPL-3.0-or-later.
- Documentation and method contributions are licensed under CC BY-SA 4.0.

The contributor does not acquire ownership of the entire updated application. The combined project contains pre-existing work and contributions from multiple rights holders.

## CC BY-SA 4.0 requirements for method adaptations

A person who shares an adapted version of PlotPickle’s CC-licensed instructional material must:

- identify the original creator or designated attribution party;
- retain supplied copyright and licence notices;
- link to or provide the CC BY-SA 4.0 licence;
- indicate that changes were made;
- retain indications of earlier modifications where applicable; and
- license the adapted material under CC BY-SA 4.0 or an officially compatible licence.

Attribution must not imply endorsement.

## User-created and AI-assisted work

PlotPickle’s licences do not attach themselves to the content produced with the tool.

A Human may choose traditional all-rights-reserved copyright, a Creative Commons licence, a collaboration agreement, a studio contract, or another lawful arrangement for rights they hold in their own story. PlotPickle does not make that decision for the Human.

PlotPickle does not determine or promise whether any particular material, including AI-assisted output, qualifies for copyright protection. Rights may depend on Human authorship, source material, provider terms, collaboration agreements, and applicable law.

## Collaboration records

For accepted PlotPickle contributions, attribution may be maintained through:

- Git commit history;
- contributor files;
- release notes;
- acknowledgements;
- issue or pull-request history; or
- a separate written agreement.

The project owner and contributor may agree on another reasonable attribution format when needed.
