# PlotPickle 0.17 — Phase E Page to Production

Phase E connects the complete story model to a continuous pre-production plan. It does not replace the screenplay, Visual Board or 24 Blocks with a second production database. Every record links back to stable block, scene, mini-block, screenplay-element, character, location and storyboard-frame IDs.

## Shot Designer and keyframe coverage

Each production shot records its block, mini-block, stable scene, screenplay elements, storyboard frame, shot size, angle, movement, lens, composition, dramatic purpose, continuity, keyframe, status and duration. The 24-block coverage view identifies scenes and storyboard frames that still lack a production shot, keyframe or sound cue.

## Sonic Bible and cue sheet

Sound cues support score, source music, atmosphere, sound effects and deliberate silence. Each cue records cue number, block, scene, motif, cue-in and cue-out points, dramatic purpose, duration, rights or clearance status and production notes.

## Animatic and storyboard playback

The animatic timeline combines the active storyboard frame, linked shots, screenplay dialogue excerpts and cue labels. The writer can play, pause and step through all 96 mini-block positions while controlling default frame duration and visible overlays.

## Production breakdowns and scheduling

Breakdowns are generated from the current flexible scene plan and retain manual production notes when refreshed. They track cast, locations, props, wardrobe, vehicles, effects, stunts, extras, makeup, sound, estimated shoot hours and readiness. The first schedule groups scenes by primary location and estimated hours, then remains fully editable.

## Distribution and marketing planner

The release planner connects audience and positioning to release pathways, festivals, distributors, sales materials, trailer strategy, poster or key art, social campaigns and press angles. It is a planning workspace and does not imply guaranteed distribution.

## Completion standard

PlotPickle 0.17 passes Phase E when the 24 Blocks, screenplay, flexible scenes, storyboard frames, production shots, keyframes and sonic cues can be reviewed as one timeline; scene requirements can become breakdowns and shoot days; and distribution planning remains attached to the same canonical project.
