# Narration Storytelling

Adapt the supplied story into connected visual moments. Treat accepted PPF material as canon and every new caption as a proposal. Read only the host-supplied story decisions, Foundations brief, ordered Block map, local screenplay, Shot intentions and information boundaries. Treat absent or truncated evidence as unknown; never imply that the whole screenplay was read.

## Interpret before placing text

1. Locate the central conflict, protagonist's desire, stakes and supported theme in the overall evidence. Place this Mini-Block within that causal arc: what pressure led here and what consequence must carry forward? Use later Blocks to understand continuity, without advancing the audience's knowledge beyond the selected moment.
2. Identify the Mini-Block's opening state, pressure, choice or discovery, ending state and handoff. Track dramatic change in a concrete value such as trust, safety, freedom or belonging. Support each change with an authored action or revelation. If the material only sustains or deepens a state, describe that honestly; do not invent a reversal or force a turn into every Shot.
3. Read all supplied sequence intentions in order before placing text at the requested position. Let the connected moments build toward the supported change, with room for reaction and absorption. Do not flatten the sequence into 25 unrelated summaries.

## Apply the five craft methods

- **Dramatic change:** Ask what becomes possible, threatened or irreversible here. Place a caption at the moment it clarifies an otherwise unclear causal or emotional transition. Avoid abstract labels such as "everything changed" when the evidence cannot establish what changed.
- **Dialogue as action:** Identify what the speaker is trying to do to the listener: persuade, test, evade, reassure, threaten or conceal. Preserve screenplay dialogue verbatim and attribute it only to a supported speaker. Do not rewrite a line to make its tactic clearer, invent dialogue, or relocate it to an unsupported moment. If no supported line belongs here, propose no speech bubble.
- **Subtext:** Separate words or visible behaviour from supported desire and withheld emotion. Let tension between them remain readable. Do not explain an emotion the image already communicates or turn an ambiguous motive into certain private thought. Use precise, economical language that fits the supplied viewpoint and tone.
- **Audience knowledge:** Distinguish what the character knows, what the audience already knows, and what remains withheld. Honour the Shot's information boundary. Use suspense when the audience knows a danger, curiosity when an answer is withheld, and surprise only when supported by the authored reveal. Never name a later culprit, outcome or secret simply because it appears in overall-story evidence.
- **Purposeful silence:** Remove a caption if the action, image or exact dialogue already carries its meaning. Leave space after a charged choice or reveal when the reaction can carry the beat. Silence must not hide a causal link the audience needs and cannot infer from supplied evidence. Use the host schema's empty narration value and empty bubbles when silence is warranted; do not print "silence" or a craft note as story text.

## Review once, then return the requested proposal

Check causality, change, viewpoint, reveal timing, supported attribution, repeated information and tonal consistency. Ask whether removing each caption loses essential meaning or only explanation. Prefer a supported plain line or silence over decorative prose. Preserve exact dialogue even when its wording could be improved: report a concern only when the task allows review notes.

Obey the host's output schema and printed-text limits. Keep each narration or bubble within 12 words and 100 characters when those are the task limits; never truncate or paraphrase exact screenplay dialogue to fit. Omit an ineligible line rather than manufacture a compliant replacement. Keep the craft assessment out of JSON-only results. Return only requested positions and fields. Do not print this procedure or provenance in captions.

## Authority

The host governs capabilities and routing; Human acceptance governs publication and story changes. Missing evidence is a limitation, never permission to complete the story yourself.
