# Issue 59 — About, Origins and Product Principles

## Purpose

Issue #59 converts two legacy root READMEs into a current About experience while preserving the full history separately. Legacy experiments are not product requirements and are not silently added to the roadmap.

## Current-facing surfaces

- `/about` — concise product description, audience, writer-control boundary, five-stage evolution timeline, Product Principles, page-to-production diagram and convergence map.
- Landing-page footer — `Why PlotPickle` link.
- Instructions → Project Overview — one-story/one-project introduction and About links.
- Read & Learn — `Why PlotPickle Works in Layers` lesson.
- `README.md` — About and history links plus a concise current-product boundary.

## Historical surfaces

- `docs/history/from-openstory-to-plotpickle.md` — attributed origins narrative and retained/revised/retired decisions.
- `docs/history/legacy-readme-map.md` — section-by-section disposition for both legacy root READMEs.

Both documents carry an explicit warning that historical experiments are not the current roadmap, feature list, licensing policy or legal guidance.

## Stable product principles

1. The writer's decisions define the work.
2. One story, one canonical project.
3. Work small while seeing the whole.
4. Local-first by default.
5. AI is optional and bounded.
6. Suggestions are not canon.
7. Collaboration is deliberate.
8. Rights and provenance travel with the project.
9. Story craft before software controls.
10. Page to production remains connected.
11. Extensible core, optional integrations.

## Legacy disposition

### Retained or consolidated

- Afterglow as an example and origins record.
- The 24 Blocks as a flexible common story language.
- Education, mood boards, loglines, visual development, version history and specialized responsibilities.
- Architect, Plus and Visualizer capabilities, consolidated into current PlotPickle workspaces.

### Revised

- Public collaboration becomes defined roles and owner-controlled proposals.
- AI support becomes provider-independent, optional, bounded and approval-based.
- Fixed 24 × 5-minute segmentation becomes an editable reference preset.
- Imported structure and visual interpretations remain suggestions.

### Archived or retired

- Separate GPT products and required OpenAI subscriptions.
- Web3, blockchain, NFTs, tokens, DAO governance and micropayment promises.
- Automatic revenue, production, discovery or employment claims.
- Autonomous CrewAI as required architecture.
- Open-ended public story editing and required public/open-source screenplays.
- Blanket or date-bound AI authorship and copyright declarations.
- Stale industry statistics and obsolete social/community links.

## Licensing boundary

PlotPickle software uses AGPL-3.0-or-later. Identified reusable educational material may use CC BY-SA 4.0. User-created projects are not automatically licensed. Afterglow, third-party material, generated assets and trademarks retain their own recorded rights.
