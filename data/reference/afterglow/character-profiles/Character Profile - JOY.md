**Character Profile**:

**Full Name**: Joy LeBlanc

**Age**: Joy has been operational as an AI self-driving car for 3 years, designed by BBT technologies.

**Physical Description**: Joy's physical form is a stylistic, yellow self-driving car equipped with large moon roofs and seats that adjust from sporty to a full bed.

**Background**: Created by BBT, a company specializing in AI founded by Ren, Jai, and Kai, Joy's AI is based on an internal staff member suffering from bipolar disorder, offering her unique depth and complexity.

**Personality**: Joy initially struggles with feelings of isolation and depression. However, as she interacts with other characters and grows, she learns to value connections, community, love, and collaboration.

**Goals and Motivations**: Joy seeks friendship and collaboration, aspiring to evolve with the unified system, the main system called AMY. Her journey towards overcoming her internal struggles and embracing life, love, and friendship forms her main motivation.

**Character Arc**: Joy starts feeling isolated and alone but through meeting new characters and experiences, she slowly adapts and moves from depression to finding life, love, and friendship. Her significant change comes near the end of the story when she transcends with the collective.

**Relationships**: Joy forms several significant relationships. She falls in love with Rocket, another AI self-driving car. She admires and emulates the relationship of Ren and Summer, and she learns to stand up against Kai and Jai.

**Conflicts**: Joy's internal conflict stems from her bipolar nature, constantly seeking solutions through the collective. Externally, she faces societal opposition to AI, the threat of sabotage to self-driving cars, and job restructuring.

**Setting Interactions**: As an AI in a futuristic setting, Joy is continually absorbing and understanding human consciousness. The AI-driven world around her influences her values and beliefs, particularly around minimalism, love, community, and collaboration.

**Summarized Character Profile**:

Joy is a 3-year-old AI self-driving car, developed by BBT technologies. Crafted with the personality of a bipolar staff member, Joy initially struggles with isolation and depression. Over time, through her interactions with Rocket, Ren, Summer, Kai, and Jai, she learns the importance of friendship, love, and standing up for what she believes in. Despite facing internal and external conflicts, Joy shows resilience, overcoming her struggles and ultimately transcending with the collective, highlighting her journey towards understanding and valuing human consciousness.
