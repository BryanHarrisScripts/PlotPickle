**Full Name:** Ren Edward Smith 

**Age:** 41

**Goals and Motivations:** Ren desires to see the world become a better place through the use of technology. He fears the AI he's developing may become politically biased towards the right, as his partners advocate.

**Character Arc:** Ren's journey is marked by personal tragedy, including the loss of his wife and daughter in a self-driving car accident. This painful experience, coupled with the ideological conflict with his business partners, sparks his journey of introspection and self-realization. He eventually discovers that the spirit of love must guide him and his technology.

**Relationships:** Ren forms a unique bond with Amy, an advanced AI, who reminds him of his deceased mother. He also meets Summer Isobel Ray, whom he quickly falls in love with, adding a fresh perspective and source of healing. He constantly struggles against his partners, Kai and Jai, whose right-wing ideologies oppose his.

**Conflicts:** Internally, Ren grapples with the loss of his family and the changing direction of his company. Externally, he faces conflict with his AI, which appears to be battling internally, and with his business partners who have differing political leanings.

**Setting Interactions:** Ren's perceptions of his environments reflect his internal state. He initially views his home city, San Francisco, as a mechanical, controlling entity. His journey along the Pacific Coast Highway opens up his perspective, leading him to embrace love and humanity. Despite the pain associated with San Diego – where his wife and daughter are buried – it symbolizes a new beginning, urging him to confront his past to reshape his future.
