# Issue 55 — PlotPickle Core Curriculum and Learning Router

## Purpose

The legacy General archive is the source foundation for PlotPickle's existing fourteen broad learning modules. Issue #55 does not create a duplicate General collection. It makes those existing modules the visible orientation spine that routes writers into the deeper learning systems delivered in issues #48–#54.

## Five-stage core spine

1. Find the Story
   - The Pitch
   - Tropes and Genres
   - Story Structures: Screenplays to Improv
2. Build the Story World
   - The Writing Process
   - Concept to Final Draft
   - World-Building
   - Story Bible: Character
   - The Story Bible
3. Write the Movie
   - The Pickle Draft
   - Script Formatting
4. Learn and Diagnose
   - Books, Screenplays and Deliberate Study
   - Screenplay Challenges Guide
5. Prepare and Work Responsibly
   - The Film Industry
   - Responsible AI-Assisted Writing

The stage order is advisory. There are no locked prerequisites and writers remain free to open any lesson or workspace.

## Entry routes

The router offers six editable, local-first recommendations:

- I have an idea
- I am starting a new screenplay
- I already have a screenplay
- I am stuck on one area
- I am revising a complete draft
- I am preparing to collaborate or share

The focused-problem route adds character, dialogue, scene, structure, pacing, theme, world, collaboration, formatting and AI destinations.

A route is stored as a local preference using the active project ID. It is not canonical story data and does not lock any feature.

## Progress boundaries

Generic reading and project application are intentionally separate.

- `Read` is stored in browser-local learning preferences.
- `Exercise attempted`, `Applied to project` and `Revisit` are encoded in existing project review threads.
- Project records use the marker `PLOTPICKLE_CORE_LEARNING_RECORD`.
- Project evidence travels with the `.ppf` project.
- Nothing is shared unless the writer deliberately shares the project or a proposal.

This preserves PlotPickle schema 1.7 and avoids account or external-service dependencies.

## Core-to-workspace panels

Every one of the fourteen modules now has router metadata for:

- Understand — the foundational concept
- See it — the existing PlotPickle worked example and current evidence
- Try it — the active-project exercise
- Apply it — a direct workspace
- Check it — a diagnostic, report or evidence surface
- Go deeper — a specialized collection from #48–#54
- Recommended before — advisory only
- Useful after — advisory only
- Common next problem — a likely branching question

## Evidence-based recommendations

Recommendations inspect active project evidence such as:

- logline and audience promise;
- genre metadata;
- Block goals and choices;
- revision notes;
- world rules and visual language;
- character wants and needs;
- Story Threads and continuity notes;
- screenplay element counts and internal-state language;
- scene pivots;
- rights ownership and pitch-package readiness;
- AI provenance records.

The router explains why a lesson may help, shows the evidence and asks a question. It does not issue mandatory warnings or universal scores.
