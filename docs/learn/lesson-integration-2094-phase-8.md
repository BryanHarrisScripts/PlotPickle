## Result

All ten Phase 8 lessons were learner-sufficient before changes. No learner-facing curriculum rewrite was justified.

### Revision

`Structure and causality audit` already keeps the task evidence-first and bounded: trace objective, opposition, choice, consequence and handoff; preserve writer intent; reject formulaic beat enforcement, invented twists and chronology-as-causality.

`Scene purpose and turn audit` already tests entry condition, objective, opposition, tactics, turn, consequence and handoff before any rewrite. It correctly rejects scene summary, dialogue added in place of function and volume-only conflict.

`Conflict, stakes and escalation audit` already distinguishes escalation from spectacle. Available choices, concrete cumulative costs, adaptive opposition and causally earned alternatives are the governing tests; invented villains, arbitrary death/trauma and bigger explosions are explicitly rejected.

`Pacing and repetition pass` carries the attached `Critique and Pacing` and `Redundancy and Streamlining` sources. Durable source teaching about repeated information, redundant scenes/exchanges, delayed turns, pacing plateaus and evidence-based compression is already present. Repeated-word polish, action-line density, dialogue craft, character consistency, visual/sensory balance and cultural/inclusivity review belong to their specialist lessons rather than being duplicated here. Legacy percentage/character-count targets, producer-appeal claims and automatic rewrite framing are rejected or historical.

`Formatting and readability pass` already separates technical readability from creative change, uses a bounded checklist and rejects one house style as universal law, arbitrary length targets and wording changes during a format audit. Recipient-specific or changing delivery requirements remain outside frozen craft authority.

`Pitch, synopsis and audience-language pass` already protects the approved story promise, unsupported claims and destination-specific audience language while keeping screenplay text untouched. Disclosure and spoiler choices remain purpose-dependent rather than a universal no-spoiler rule.

`Run the Essential Craft Audit` already provides the full fourteen-step evidence sequence from story promise through revision priorities. It separates symptom from root cause, treats findings as questions rather than verdicts, preserves the original and explicitly rejects universal scores, formula checklists and claims of an objectively perfect final draft.

### Responsible AI

`Responsible AI-Assisted Writing` carries seven historical sources. The current lesson already absorbs their durable core: define the task, bound context, state constraints, evaluate alternatives, preserve human authorship/approval, protect privacy and credentials, retain provenance, verify rights/consent, review bias/cultural assumptions and keep a complete no-AI route. Structural craft prompts such as Principle of Three and broad screenplay-technique catalogues are better owned by Structure/Revision. Brand-specific ChatGPT wording, old training-cutoff or token-limit assumptions, product-specific capability claims, blockchain/NFT proposals and time-sensitive copyright/legal conclusions remain historical or require current authoritative verification rather than being frozen into teaching.

`AI at Every Structural Level` is source-free and already teaches the durable method directly: match the task to beat/scene/mini-block/Block/sequence/act scope, send the smallest useful context pack, preserve explicit canon locks and keep generated → reviewed → revised → approved → canonical states separate. AI remains optional and the no-AI diagnostic path remains complete.

`AI, GitHub and Public Publishing as Optional Tools` carries the historical `Structure, Characters, Conflict, & Beyond` prompt source. The current lesson correctly separates AI assistance, private version control, public repositories, social serialization and open licensing as different optional choices. Its source's durable craft questions are already owned by specialist craft/revision lessons; fixed five-page/token-limit wrappers, ChatGPT-specific rewrite instructions, character-count targets and commercial-appeal claims are not current authority. Platform terms, rights and legal classification remain matters for current authoritative verification.
