# Opening Move

Opening Move is PlotPickle's first-contact diagnostic inside Block 1. It asks what the audience begins tracking, feeling, predicting and needing before the story explains the whole situation.

The seven effects are Anchor, Grip, Compass, Question, Imprint, Echo and Handoff. They draw from the existing protagonist, first scene, hook, world rules, theme, audience question, ending and final block.

A quiet action, visual contradiction, line, sound or behavioural detail may perform several effects at once. The diagnostic passes when the opening creates forward motion, establishes a promise the rest of the film keeps, and cannot be removed without damaging the story's causal, emotional, visual or thematic design.
