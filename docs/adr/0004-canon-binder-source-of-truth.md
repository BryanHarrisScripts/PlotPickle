# ADR-0004: Canon Binder is the approved source of truth

Status: Accepted

## Context

Story, screenplay, character, production, import and AI tools can otherwise create competing versions of shared facts.

## Decision

The Canon Binder owns approved cross-module facts, rights, research, chronology, continuity and decisions. Other modules may propose changes but do not silently establish Canon.

## Consequences

Imports and AI output carry review states and provenance. Reports read Canon rather than duplicating it. Users can trace what was approved, rejected or superseded and why.
