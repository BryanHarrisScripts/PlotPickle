## Batch result — Lessons 11–20

Lessons 11–16 remain high-fidelity. Lessons 17–20 correctly avoid becoming encyclopedic catch-alls, but the old decision to mark large Character bodies as `ALREADY_COVERED` was too permissive. The concepts were distributed, yet several useful explanations and established terms were not carried forward explicitly.

Required restoration targets for Lessons 21–26:

- **21 — Map the Inner Journey Without Forcing It:** preserve the useful Act 1–4 question bank as optional movement/checkpoint prompts; remove mandatory Block placement and mandatory beats.
- **22 — Join Inner and Outer Conflict:** restore traditional/search aliases (`Man vs Man`, `Man vs Self/Himself`, `Man vs Machine`, `Man vs Other`) beside modern neutral labels and restore the useful subtypes/examples of interpersonal, internal, societal/system and technology conflict.
- **23 — Build Opposition as a Competing Worldview:** retain explicit thesis / antithesis / synthesis terminology as an optional dialectical lens; deepen antagonist, foil and rival distinctions where useful.
- **24 — Design Relationships That Change Both People:** restore durable relationship lenses such as chemistry, mentor–pupil dynamics, loyalty/betrayal, family pressure, companionship/rivalry and power dynamics as optional lenses rather than formulas.
- **25 — Give Every Character a Playable Voice:** preserve source factors such as background, education, profession, worldview, rhythm and word choice while avoiding accent/demographic caricature.
- **26 — Design the Cast as a Dramatic System:** restore recognizable archetype vocabulary as optional/searchable aliases, plus durable teaching on minor characters, memorable entrances/exits, non-human characters and the useful Character-pattern library. Profitability claims and borrowed-title proof do not survive.
