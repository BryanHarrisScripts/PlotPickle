# Opening Move

Opening Move is PlotPickle's first-contact design layer. It lives inside Act I Launch and gives Block 1 a precise job: create the audience's first useful relationship with the story.

It does not prescribe a type of opening, a required character, or a fixed amount of spectacle. A quiet action, a startling event, a visual contradiction, a line, a sound, or a behavioural detail can perform several effects at once. The test is what the audience begins noticing, feeling, predicting, and needing—not whether the writer checks nine separate boxes.

## Two opening decisions

| Field | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| Entry strategy | Chooses whose experience, pressure, or vulnerability becomes the audience's doorway and explains why it suits what viewers already know. |
| First signal | Defines the first image, action, sound, or line the audience can read as meaningful. |

## Seven first-contact effects

| Effect | Audience result |
| --- | --- |
| Anchor | Gives viewers a person, force, value, or vulnerability to track. |
| Grip | Creates pressure, contrast, or an information gap that makes the next moment necessary. |
| Compass | Lets viewers infer a relevant world rule and the story's point of view without stopping for a lecture. |
| Question | Starts an active uncertainty that feeds The Pickle without answering it. |
| Imprint | Plants the story's blend of tone, theme, rhythm, and creative personality. |
| Echo | Plans how the ending will transform the meaning of the opening signal. |
| Handoff | Creates the causal, emotional, or visual bridge that makes the opening inseparable from the main story. |

## Architecture connections

Opening Move draws from existing PlotPickle systems rather than competing with them:

- Characters supplies the behaviour, contradiction, capability, or vulnerability used by Anchor.
- World supplies the rule or contrast carried by Compass.
- Foundations and Theme shape Imprint.
- The Pickle supplies the longer uncertainty seeded by Question.
- Block 1's Primary Presence and Revealing Contrast turn the plan into action.
- The final blocks deliver Echo by repeating, reversing, answering, or recontextualizing the first signal.

## Diagnostic pass

An effective Opening Move should pass four checks:

1. The audience can feel forward motion before it knows the whole situation.
2. Story information arrives through choices, behaviour, environment, and contrast whenever possible.
3. The opening establishes a promise the rest of the story actually keeps.
4. Removing the opening would damage the story's causal, emotional, visual, or thematic design.

If an effect is deliberately absent, another choice may perform its function later or more powerfully. The explanation matters more than conformity.

## Workspace integration

Story Planner stores Opening Move as prefixed fields inside `development.actOne`. Visual Board surfaces the first signal and Grip beside Block 1 together with its launch signals. Instructions explains the audience effect of each field rather than presenting a rigid scene formula.

Opening Move was introduced in schema version 1.4.0 and remains intact in the current schema. Version 1.5.0 imports projects from versions 1.0.0 through 1.4.0 with blank defaults for later architecture without losing existing project data.
