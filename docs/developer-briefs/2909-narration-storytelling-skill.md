## Forward-test evidence

Two independent task trials used the skill with invented raw evidence and no suggested answers. Neither edited code or called a product provider.

Trial A: courier Mara accepts Ivo's risky help. The audience sees an unidentified watcher; a later employer-as-captor ending is supplied only for continuity. Four panels returned empty narration; only panel 2 carried IVO's exact line, "Take my coat. They are looking for yours." Panels 3 and 4 kept the readable emotional action and unresolved route silent. Result: no future reveal, invented route, changed dialogue or redundant caption; all outputs within bounds.

Trial B: Veda deliberately leaves the dusk bell silent, defying a previously unseen council order. The future overthrow remains withheld. Returned caption: "The council ordered the bell rung every dusk. Tonight, Veda refused." (11 words, 68 characters.) Result: supplies essential authored context the image cannot convey, preserves the current choice and withholds future outcome.

These trials test transferable procedure on contrasting tasks, including when silence helps and when it would obscure causality. Real local-model narration quality remains Human acceptance under #2855.
