## Result

All ten Phase 7 lessons were learner-sufficient before changes. No learner-facing curriculum rewrite was justified.

### Dialogue

`Subtext, status and silence pass` is intentionally narrow and already provides a bounded diagnosis/alternative workflow with evidence, canon-risk separation and explicit approval. It does not need to absorb the deeper Dialogue curriculum.

`Dialogue Is Action` already teaches objective, tactic, resistance, information/leverage, spoken or silent action, changed exit condition and crafted naturalism. The attached Realistic Dialogue source's durable teaching is present without treating naturalism as literal transcription.

`Build a Playable Voiceprint` already teaches voice through perception, rhythm, vocabulary, metaphor, emotional access, status, persuasion and relationship-specific variation. The attached Distinctive Voices and Reveal Character sources are absorbed while accent/demographic shorthand remains rejected and informed human review remains explicit.

`Write Subtext and Withheld Information` already teaches public topic, private subject, protected fact, cost of direct speech, indirect tactic, audience knowledge and reveal/misread. The attached Subtext source is fully represented without making every line cryptic.

`Create Conflict Without Constant Arguments` already teaches incompatible objectives, knowledge imbalance, status, affection, refusal, delay, leverage and tactic changes. The attached Conflict source is absorbed while shouting/constant argument is explicitly rejected as a universal model.

`Balance Speech, Silence and Physical Action` already combines visible behaviour, reaction, task, interruption, distance, silence and accurate screenplay form. The Art of Silence, Balancing Action and Tags and Beats sources are represented directly; broader screen-evidence craft remains with Visual Storytelling.

`Handle Exposition, World and Genre` already attaches information to conflict, task, urgency, misunderstanding and consequence, reveals world rules through use, and treats genre as pressure rather than a voice stereotype. The Different Genres source is sufficiently integrated.

`Shape the Exchange and Scene Turn` is source-free in the canonical topic file and already teaches entry tension, tactic, resistance, tactic change, reveal/misinterpretation, status shift, irreversible action, turn and exit consequence. Historical Conflict/Realistic Dialogue aliases are retrieval links, not additional required source records.

`Revise for Voice, Purpose and Performance` already turns Dialogue Pitfalls and Refining Dialogue into twelve bounded revision passes, read-aloud evidence and optional/human-approved AI assistance. Diagnostic counts are not grades and synthetic voice use remains permission-bound.

### Revision

`Diagnose without rewriting` already teaches symptom-versus-root-cause diagnosis, evidence-first questions, narrow canonical scope, optional suggestions and explicit approval. Its three historical Individual Prompt sources contain broad critical/intermediate/fine-tuning prompts; durable diagnosis concepts are already present or owned by later specialist Revision lessons. Legacy ChatGPT wrappers, automatic rewriting and cultural/factual certification remain historical, rejected or routed to Responsible AI/human verification.
