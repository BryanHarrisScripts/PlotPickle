## 62 — Dialogue Is Action

Original `Realistic Dialogue.md` and broad Dialogue prompt checked directly.

- `PRESERVED`: objective, tactic, resistance, information/leverage, spoken or silent action, changed exit condition and the distinction between crafted naturalism and literal transcription.
- `RESTORE_REFERENCE`: practical naturalism tools from the source — listen to real conversations for rhythm/pattern, notice interruptions and incomplete sentences, use contractions when character/context supports them, and revise a sample exchange by cutting unnecessary words and adding purposeful interruption or nonverbal behaviour.
- `PRESERVED`: filler such as ums/ahs may be observed but should be selected rather than copied automatically.
- `REJECTED`: realism as verbatim transcription or one universal speech style.

## 66 — Balance Speech, Silence and Physical Action

Original `Balancing Action.md`, `Tags and Beats.md` and `Art of Silence.md` checked directly.

- `PRESERVED`: visible behaviour, tasks, reaction, interruption, entrance/exit, distance, overlap, unfinished thoughts and silence can replace or transform speech.
- `MODERNIZED_WITH_ALIAS`: restore `action beat` as the source/general-writing term for a small action around dialogue that can clarify speaker, reveal character/emotion or regulate rhythm; in screenplay form this normally appears as an action line rather than prose narration.
- `MODERNIZED_WITH_ALIAS`: preserve `dialogue tag` (`he said`, `she asked`) as general prose terminology while explicitly teaching that screenplay character cues/action lines usually perform speaker identification instead.
- `PRESERVED`: silence can create tension, reveal character, carry subtext or affect pacing; intentionality matters more than adding generic pauses.

## 67 — Handle Exposition, World and Genre

Original `Different Genres.md` checked directly.

- `PRESERVED`: information should attach to conflict, task, urgency, misunderstanding, world-rule use and consequence; genre shapes pressure and audience expectation rather than dictating a voice stereotype.
- `RESTORE_REFERENCE`: the original concrete genre vocabulary should remain visible as possibilities rather than rules:
  - Comedy — wordplay, puns, quick exchange, comic timing, character quirks, situational comedy and absurdity.
  - Drama — complex emotion, sustained conversation and philosophical/moral questions may become prominent; formality is optional, not required.
  - Horror — fear, denial, partial/incomplete knowledge and description of uncertain or threatening events can build dread.
  - Science fiction — technical terms, invented/future slang and large questions about technology, society, space or humanity can appear when character/world evidence supports them.
  - Romance — intimacy, desire, vulnerability, avoidance, conflict and subtext can carry attraction and changing relationship stakes.
- `REJECTED`: comedy must be banter, drama must be formal, horror characters must verbalize fear, science fiction must use jargon or romance must be emotionally explicit.

## 69 — Revise for Voice, Purpose and Performance

Original `Dialogue Pitfalls.md` and `Refining Dialogue.md` checked directly; Different Genres teaching remains owned by Lesson 67.

- `PRESERVED`: diagnose on-the-nose dialogue, unnatural phrasing, exposition dumps, weak voice separation and conflict/purpose problems through focused passes.
- `RESTORE_REFERENCE`: retain the original practical revision sequence explicitly — read dialogue aloud; check character-voice consistency; cut unnecessary words; clarify the purpose of each line/exchange; get outside feedback and compare what listeners understood or felt.
- `PRESERVED`: revision is iterative and performance-aware rather than a one-pass polish or automatic rewrite.
- `REJECTED`: diagnostic counts as grades, synthetic performance as authenticity proof or every short line as automatically better.

## 70 — Diagnose without rewriting

Original `Individual Critical`, `Individual Intermediate` and `Individual Fine-Tuning` prompt sources checked directly.

- `PRESERVED`: symptom versus root cause, evidence-first questions, narrow scope, optional suggestions and explicit approval are the durable modern form of the old prompt library.
- `RESTORE_REFERENCE`: preserve the three legacy diagnostic levels as recognizable scope vocabulary rather than losing the original organization:
  - Most Critical — story/structure, emotion/conflict, character development, dialogue, theme/irony, subplots, inciting incidents/twists, resolution/climax, visual imagery, antagonist, relationships, introductions, motivations and backstory.
  - Intermediate — scene/narrative analysis, exposition, genre/world-building, motifs/symbolism, nonlinear form, cinematic/visual language, tone, humour/surprise, sound, genre conventions, pathos/catharsis, ensemble dynamics, culture/time, continuity, sensory detail, foreshadowing and subtext.
  - Fine-Tuning — pacing/rhythm, voice-over/narration, formatting/style, rewriting/editing, title significance, action description, diction/language, dialogue authenticity, representation, feedback/revision, pitching/marketing and audience perspective.
- `MOVED_ELSEWHERE`: each craft topic belongs to its specialist lesson for deep teaching; Lesson 70 keeps the diagnostic index and routing function rather than becoming a second 96-lesson curriculum.
- `HISTORICAL_ONLY`: ChatGPT token-limit framing, fixed 500/1500-character prompt wrappers and “copy code box” instructions.
- `REJECTED`: automatic full-script rewriting, invented cultural authenticity, guaranteed producer appeal, universal audience optimization or automatically inserting twists/symbols/humour because a checklist asks about them.

The modern Dialogue system is substantively strong, but the stricter pass identified useful original vocabulary and exercises that should be made explicit: naturalism observation/contractions, verbal sparring/subtle disagreement, action beats/dialogue tags, concrete genre dialogue possibilities and a practical read-aloud revision sequence. No source record is removed and learner-source chevrons remain visible during the fidelity pass.
