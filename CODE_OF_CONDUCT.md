# Code of conduct

PlotPickle welcomes respectful participation from writers, developers, artists, educators and reviewers.

Participants must:

- discuss ideas and changes without personal attacks or harassment;
- respect privacy, authorship, attribution and creative ownership;
- never publish another person's story material, identity, contact information or credentials without permission;
- avoid discriminatory, threatening, sexualized or deliberately disruptive conduct; and
- follow maintainer decisions about safety, scope and repository access.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, comments or access that violate these expectations.

For a sensitive conduct report, contact the repository owner privately. If no private contact channel is available, use a private security advisory titled "Conduct report" and do not include unnecessary personal or story information in the initial report.
