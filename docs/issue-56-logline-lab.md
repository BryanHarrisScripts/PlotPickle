# Issue 56 — Purpose-aware Logline Lab

PlotPickle’s existing Logline Workshop already provided guided story-engine questions, temporary previews, candidate approval, anchored feedback, canonical synchronization and local-only operation. Issue 56 refines that implementation rather than creating a second broad Loglines curriculum.

## Purpose-aware model

The Lab supports one approved primary logline and labelled variants for:

- development;
- submission;
- pitch deck;
- public teaser;
- collaborator brief;
- a writer-defined custom purpose.

Purpose and intended audience determine what the sentence must communicate, what nearby pitch material can carry and what information may be deliberately withheld.

## Transparent deterministic shapes

The no-AI workshop can build alternatives using:

- causal engine;
- irony or contradiction;
- relationship pressure;
- world-rule pressure;
- mystery or thriller;
- dual-protagonist or ensemble;
- character-first pressure.

Each alternative exposes the shape, confirmed project ingredients, omissions and any scaffolding assumption. The writer can edit every candidate before saving.

## Evidence instead of quality scoring

The former binary 20-point score and external-quality bands are replaced with three evidence groups:

1. Core dramatic engine
2. Promise and distinction
3. Clarity and delivery

Each item is labelled as supported in the sentence, present only in the project, intentionally omitted or unclear and worth reviewing. Word count, proper names, urgency, irony and other enhancements remain advisory.

## Candidate and approval boundaries

Optional metadata preserves purpose, audience, shape, ingredients, rationale, evidence, omissions, notes, source type and review status while remaining backward-compatible with older candidate records.

Imported screenplay candidates remain labelled suggestions with cited screenplay evidence and uncertain interpretations. They do not become primary automatically.

Approval is selective. The writer chooses whether to update:

- the primary story logline;
- the one-sentence pitch;
- the pitch package;
- a labelled purpose variant;
- a named revision snapshot preserving the previous primary.

## Learning connection

`Loglines That Carry the Movie` is a focused deep dive beneath the existing Pitch foundation. It is searchable in Read & Learn using both current terminology and legacy phrases such as `The Art of Crafting Loglines`, `20-step logline guide`, `perfect logline` and `logline deconstruction`.

## Local-first contract

The complete Lab works manually and locally without AI, an account or an external service. Optional AI may propose alternatives only from bounded confirmed ingredients and remains temporary until the writer saves and approves a candidate.
