# Scene Pulse

Scene Pulse is PlotPickle's scene-level diagnostic. The 24 Blocks define larger causal movements; Scene Pulse tests whether each flexible scene creates playable pressure and a change of its own.

The diagnostic examines Scene Identity, Pressure Lock, Cut Line, Character Proof, Value Flip, Focus Signal and Handoff. It asks whether someone pursues an immediate result, whether an incompatible force resists it, why the participants cannot disengage, where the scene should enter and leave, what pivot changes the tactic, which live value changes, and what consequence drives the next movement.

Scene Pulse is not prescriptive. A short scene may perform several functions with one action, while a set piece may carry several prepared turns. The requirement is audience-facing evidence that the scene earns its screen time, reveals character under pressure and changes what can happen next.
